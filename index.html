Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my tracking username?

Launchpad Studio sends it to you when your project starts. It usually looks like your company name with dashes, for example rico-motors. Can't find it? Contact us and we'll resend it.

How does the concept preview PIN work?

Your concept preview is protected by a 6-digit PIN. You get 5 views, and every attempt, right or wrong, uses one of them, so enter it carefully.

How do I request changes to my website design?

Open your project and use the Comments & Revisions section to send a message. We see it straight away and reply in the same thread.

Can I download my quote or invoice?

Yes. Every quote and invoice appears under Invoices & Quotes with a Download button that gives you a printable PDF.
